Simple rules, visible reasons.

A complete outfit contains one top, one bottom and shoes. A jacket is optional.

  • Every piece must be available and share a style and season tag.
  • This first version allows one accent colour and one patterned piece. Blue and navy count as neutrals.
  • “Similar” means the same category, colour, fit, pattern, style and season tags. Photos are for display; they aren’t analysed.
  • Cost per wear is the price divided by the number of wears you estimate.

These rules are a starting point for exploration. They don’t judge beauty or guarantee fit, warmth or suitability.

Wardrobe, photos, saved outfits and wear logs live in this browser’s IndexedDB. Export a backup before clearing browser data or switching devices. The MCP server takes supplied garment metadata; it cannot read your browser wardrobe automatically.
